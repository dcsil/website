Raghav Vishnoi
Data Engineer
TELUS

Building Scalable Data Solutions in the Cloud

Raghav is a Data Engineer with over two years of experience building high-performance, scalable data solutions across cloud platforms (Azure, AWS) and on-prem Hadoop ecosystems. His work spans the full data lifecycle—from designing architectures and data models to implementing performance optimizations, automating quality checks, and enabling advanced analytics.

He has led the modernization of legacy PySpark and Airflow workloads, migrating large-scale pipelines off HDFS onto the cloud and re-architecting Spark jobs to optimize runtimes, materially accelerating data availability for downstream teams. The systems he builds are foundational infrastructure for the business, powering BI dashboards, ML models, and analytics platforms relied on across the organization.

About Our Speaker

Passionate about reliability and data accuracy, Raghav has delivered automated QA frameworks, anomaly detection systems, and KPI monitoring to keep data healthy and trustworthy. His cross-functional approach lets him partner effectively with analysts, data scientists, and stakeholders to turn raw data into actionable insights. Beyond core data engineering, he brings experience in data science, web development, DevOps, and test engineering, giving him the versatility to solve problems from multiple angles.

Raghav is currently a Data Engineer on the TELUS AI Accelerator team. Before joining TELUS, he was a Data Engineer at Align Technology and at Canadian Tire Corporation, and got his start as a Software and Data Engineer intern at Cardea Health, working on platforms for cardiometabolic clinical trials.

Raghav holds a Bachelor of Mathematics in Computational Mathematics and Statistics from the University of Waterloo, where he also served as a Residence Life Don, guiding and supporting a residential community through activities fostering personal and academic growth.
